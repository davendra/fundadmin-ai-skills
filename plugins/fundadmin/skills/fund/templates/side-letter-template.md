## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Partnership was formed pursuant to the Amended and Restated Agreement of Limited Partnership, dated as of [LPA Date] (as amended, restated, supplemented, or otherwise modified from time to time, the "Partnership Agreement");

WHEREAS, the Investor has agreed to make a capital commitment of $[Commitment Amount] to the Partnership pursuant to its Subscription Agreement dated [Subscription Date];

WHEREAS, as a condition to the Investor's capital commitment, the General Partner has agreed to provide the Investor with certain rights, modifications, and accommodations as set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Investor's capital commitment to the Partnership and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the General Partner and the Investor agree as follows:

---

## 1. Fee Modification

<!-- Include applicable fee modifications. Delete any subsections that do not apply. -->

### 1.1 Management Fee Reduction

Notwithstanding Section [X.X] of the Partnership Agreement, the Management Fee payable by the Investor shall be calculated as follows:

- **During the Investment Period:** [X.XX]% per annum (rather than [standard rate]%) on the Investor's Capital Commitment.
- **After the Investment Period:** [X.XX]% per annum (rather than [standard rate]%) on the Investor's Invested Capital (at cost).

[Alternative: "The Investor shall receive a management fee rebate equal to [X] basis points per annum on its Capital Commitment, payable [quarterly in arrears / offset against capital calls]."]

### 1.2 Organizational Expense Cap

The Investor's share of Organizational Expenses shall not exceed [X]% of the Investor's Capital Commitment, regardless of the overall organizational expense allocation to Limited Partners.

### 1.3 No Placement Agent Fees

The Investor confirms that no placement agent was involved in connection with its capital commitment. No placement agent fees, commissions, or similar charges shall be allocated to the Investor's Capital Account or charged against the Investor's interest in the Partnership.

---

## 2. Most Favored Nation (MFN) Rights

### 2.1 MFN Election Right

The General Partner shall provide the Investor with copies of, or a summary of the material terms of, all side letters entered into with other Limited Partners whose Capital Commitments are equal to or less than the Investor's Capital Commitment (the "MFN Side Letters"), in each case within [30/60] days following the final closing.

### 2.2 Election Period

The Investor shall have [30] days following receipt of the MFN Side Letters (the "Election Period") to elect to receive the benefit of any provisions contained in any MFN Side Letter. Such election shall be made by written notice to the General Partner specifying the provisions elected.

### 2.3 Exclusions

The following provisions are excluded from MFN election rights and shall not be available for election by the Investor:

- (a) Provisions relating to co-investment rights granted to investors with Capital Commitments in excess of $[threshold];
- (b) Provisions relating to tax structuring or regulatory accommodations specific to the applicable Limited Partner's organizational structure or jurisdiction;
- (c) Provisions relating to ERISA compliance granted to benefit plan investors;
- (d) Portfolio construction or excuse/exclusion rights granted in connection with specific regulatory, legal, or policy constraints of the applicable Limited Partner.

---

## 3. Co-Investment Rights

### 3.1 Co-Investment Opportunity

The General Partner shall offer the Investor the opportunity to co-invest alongside the Partnership in transactions where the General Partner determines, in its sole discretion, that co-investment capital is appropriate. The Investor shall have the right, but not the obligation, to co-invest up to [its pro-rata share of any such co-investment opportunity / $[amount] per transaction / [X]% of any transaction].

### 3.2 Co-Investment Terms

Co-investments shall be made on a no-fee, no-carry basis (i.e., no management fee or carried interest shall be charged on co-investment capital), unless otherwise agreed in writing at the time of the co-investment.

### 3.3 Co-Investment Allocation

If co-investment demand exceeds availability, the General Partner shall allocate co-investment opportunities among eligible Limited Partners on a [pro-rata / rotational / discretionary] basis, taking into account [each Limited Partner's Capital Commitment, prior co-investment participation, and operational capacity].

### 3.4 Notice Period

The General Partner shall provide the Investor with at least [5/10] business days' notice of any co-investment opportunity, including a summary of the investment terms, the co-investment amount available, and the expected timeline.

---

## 4. Reporting Rights

### 4.1 Enhanced Reporting

In addition to the reporting obligations set forth in Article [X] of the Partnership Agreement, the General Partner shall provide the Investor with:

- (a) Quarterly capital account statements within [45] days following the end of each fiscal quarter (rather than [60/90] days);
- (b) Quarterly portfolio company summary including revenue, EBITDA, and net debt for each portfolio investment;
- (c) Annual ESG / responsible investment report summarizing the Partnership's approach to environmental, social, and governance factors;
- (d) Notice of any material events affecting the Partnership or any portfolio company within [5] business days of occurrence, including but not limited to: key person events, material litigation, regulatory actions, or defaults.

### 4.2 Data Format

Upon request, the General Partner shall provide reporting data in machine-readable format (e.g., ILPA-compliant data templates, Excel, or CSV) in addition to PDF reports.

### 4.3 Access to Records

The Investor (and its authorized representatives) shall have the right to access the books and records of the Partnership upon reasonable notice during normal business hours, subject to reasonable confidentiality protections.

---

## 5. Transfer Rights

### 5.1 Permitted Transfers

Notwithstanding Section [X.X] of the Partnership Agreement, the Investor may transfer all or a portion of its Partnership Interest without the prior consent of the General Partner (but with prior written notice) to:

- (a) Any Affiliate of the Investor (as defined in the Partnership Agreement);
- (b) Any successor entity resulting from a reorganization, merger, or consolidation of the Investor;
- (c) Any fund or account managed by the same investment manager or investment advisor as the Investor.

### 5.2 GP Consent Standard

For transfers to parties other than those listed in Section 5.1, the General Partner shall not unreasonably withhold, condition, or delay its consent to a proposed transfer, provided that the transferee meets the qualification requirements set forth in the Partnership Agreement.

---

## 6. Key Person Provisions

### 6.1 Key Person Notification

The General Partner shall notify the Investor in writing within [5] business days of the occurrence of a Key Person Event (as defined in the Partnership Agreement) or the departure of any senior investment professional named in the Partnership Agreement or Private Placement Memorandum.

### 6.2 Additional Key Person Rights

[If the Partnership Agreement does not include a key person provision, or if the Investor requires enhanced provisions:]

In the event that [Named Person 1] and/or [Named Person 2] cease to devote substantially all of their business time and attention to the affairs of the Partnership (a "Key Person Event"), the Investor shall have the right, exercisable within [60] days following receipt of notice of such event, to [suspend its Unfunded Commitment / reduce its Unfunded Commitment by [X]% / withdraw from the Partnership with respect to its Unfunded Commitment].

---

## 7. Excuse and Exclusion Rights

### 7.1 Regulatory Excuse

The Investor shall have the right to be excused from participating in any investment that would, in the Investor's reasonable determination:

- (a) Cause the Investor to violate any applicable law, regulation, or order;
- (b) Cause the Investor to violate its governing documents or investment policies;
- (c) Result in the Investor holding a "prohibited transaction" under ERISA or Section 4975 of the Internal Revenue Code;
- (d) Cause adverse tax consequences to the Investor or its beneficiaries under applicable tax law.

### 7.2 Policy-Based Exclusion

The Investor shall have the right to be excluded from any investment in [specific sectors, geographies, or activities that conflict with the Investor's investment policies — e.g., "tobacco, firearms, thermal coal, or gambling enterprises"].

### 7.3 Excuse Procedure

The Investor shall notify the General Partner in writing within [10] business days of receiving notice of a proposed investment if it wishes to exercise its excuse or exclusion rights. The General Partner shall restructure the capital call to exclude the Investor's participation in the applicable investment without affecting the Investor's other rights or obligations under the Partnership Agreement.

---

## 8. ERISA Representations

<!-- Include this section if the Investor is a benefit plan investor subject to ERISA. -->

### 8.1 Plan Asset Acknowledgment

The General Partner acknowledges that the Investor is a "benefit plan investor" as defined in Section 3(42) of ERISA and 29 C.F.R. Section 2510.3-101(f)(2). The General Partner represents and warrants that it shall operate the Partnership such that the assets of the Partnership do not constitute "plan assets" for purposes of ERISA, including by:

- (a) Ensuring that benefit plan investor participation does not equal or exceed 25% of the value of any class of equity interests in the Partnership (the "25% Test"), calculated in accordance with 29 C.F.R. Section 2510.3-101;
- (b) Providing the Investor with annual certification of the benefit plan investor percentage within [60] days following the end of each fiscal year.

### 8.2 Prohibited Transaction Protection

The General Partner shall not cause the Partnership to engage in any transaction that would constitute a "prohibited transaction" under Section 406 of ERISA or Section 4975 of the Internal Revenue Code with respect to the Investor.

---

## 9. Confidentiality Modifications

### 9.1 Public Records Exception

Notwithstanding Section [X.X] of the Partnership Agreement, the Investor may disclose information regarding the Partnership to the extent required by applicable public records laws, including but not limited to the Freedom of Information Act, state open records laws, or similar transparency requirements applicable to the Investor as a [public pension fund / government entity / sovereign wealth fund].

### 9.2 Regulatory Disclosure

The Investor may disclose information regarding the Partnership to any regulatory authority, governmental body, or self-regulatory organization having jurisdiction over the Investor, without prior notice to or consent of the General Partner.

### 9.3 Consultant and Advisor Disclosure

The Investor may disclose information regarding the Partnership to its investment consultants, legal counsel, auditors, and other professional advisors, provided that such persons are bound by obligations of confidentiality.

---

## 10. General Provisions

### 10.1 Conflict with Partnership Agreement

In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Side Letter and the Partnership Agreement, the terms of this Side Letter shall control with respect to the Investor.

### 10.2 Severability

If any provision of this Side Letter is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

### 10.3 Entire Agreement

This Side Letter, together with the Partnership Agreement and the Investor's Subscription Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

### 10.4 Amendment

This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the General Partner and the Investor.

### 10.5 Governing Law

This Side Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the [State of Delaware / applicable jurisdiction], without regard to conflict of laws principles.

### 10.6 Counterparts

This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
