## Section 10 — Removal of General Partner

### Section 10.1 — Removal for Cause

The General Partner may be removed as general partner of the Partnership for Cause (as defined in Section 10.3) upon the affirmative vote (the "Removal Vote") of Limited Partners holding in the aggregate not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of the aggregate Capital Commitments of all Limited Partners (excluding, for purposes of computing such percentage, the Capital Commitments of any Limited Partner that (i) is an Affiliate (as defined in Section 2.1(b)) of the General Partner, (ii) has, at the time of such vote, a material financial interest in the outcome of such vote that is adverse to the interests of the other Limited Partners (as determined by the Limited Partner Advisory Committee in its reasonable discretion, subject to the right of such Limited Partner to contest such determination pursuant to the dispute resolution procedures set forth in Section 15.4, provided that the pendency of any such dispute shall not delay the Removal Vote unless a court of competent jurisdiction issues a temporary restraining order to such effect), or (iii) is, at the time of such vote, a Defaulting Limited Partner and has not cured such default pursuant to Section 3.5(a) within the applicable cure period); provided, however, that no Removal Vote shall be effective unless written notice of the proposed Removal Vote has been provided to all Limited Partners (including the General Partner) not less than thirty (30) days prior to such vote (the "Removal Notice"), which Removal Notice shall set forth in reasonable detail (x) the grounds for Cause, (y) the specific acts or omissions alleged to constitute Cause, and (z) the identity of the Limited Partners initiating the Removal Vote and their aggregate Capital Commitments; provided further that the General Partner shall have a period of thirty (30) days following receipt of the Removal Notice (the "Cure Period") to cure any act or omission alleged to constitute Cause that is capable of being cured, and if the General Partner so cures (as confirmed in writing by independent legal counsel selected by the Limited Partner Advisory Committee, whose fees shall be borne by the Partnership), the Removal Notice shall be deemed withdrawn and of no further force and effect, and no further Removal Vote may be initiated on the basis of such cured act or omission (but without prejudice to any other or future Removal Vote based on different grounds).

### Section 10.3 — Definition of Cause

For purposes of Section 10.1 and Section 10.2 (relating to removal without cause), "Cause" shall mean: (i) the General Partner's fraud, willful misconduct, or gross negligence (as determined by a final, non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, or as admitted in writing by the General Partner, it being understood that an admission made in the context of a settlement agreement entered into without admission of liability shall not constitute an admission for purposes of this clause (i) unless the settlement agreement expressly so provides); (ii) the General Partner's material breach of this Agreement (which, for the avoidance of doubt, shall not include any breach that is immaterial, technical, or the result of force majeure as defined in Section 14.9), provided that such breach has not been cured within the Cure Period specified in Section 10.1; (iii) the General Partner's or any GP Principal's conviction of (or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to) any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude, dishonesty, breach of trust, or money laundering (but not including minor traffic violations, offenses for which the General Partner or such GP Principal has received a pardon, or offenses for which the relevant statute of limitations has expired); (iv) the General Partner's insolvency, bankruptcy (whether voluntary or involuntary), or the appointment of a receiver or liquidator with respect to the General Partner (unless such appointment is contested in good faith and dismissed within sixty (60) days); or (v) any event constituting a "Key Person Event" as defined in Section 6.5 that has not been cured by the reinstatement of the Investment Period pursuant to Section 6.5(b) within the sixty (60) day reinstatement window specified therein; provided, however, that none of the events described in clauses (i) through (v) above shall constitute "Cause" to the extent that such event is solely the result of actions taken by the General Partner at the express written direction of the Limited Partner Advisory Committee pursuant to Section 11.3(b).

## Section 12 — Clawback

### Section 12.1 — Clawback Obligation

(a) If, as of the date of the final liquidating distribution of the Partnership (or, if earlier, the date of the removal of the General Partner pursuant to Section 10.1 or Section 10.2), the aggregate amount of Carried Interest (as defined in Section 8.1(d)) distributed to the General Partner and the GP Principals (collectively, the "Carry Recipients") pursuant to this Agreement exceeds the amount of Carried Interest to which the Carry Recipients would have been entitled if the Partnership's entire investment portfolio had been liquidated simultaneously on the date of determination at the then-current Fair Market Value (as determined pursuant to Section 13.1, applying the valuation methodologies set forth in Exhibit B hereto, as the same may be updated from time to time by the General Partner with the prior written consent of the Limited Partner Advisory Committee, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld) (such excess, the "Clawback Amount"), then the Carry Recipients shall, jointly and severally, pay to the Partnership an amount equal to the Clawback Amount, net of (i) any income taxes actually paid by the Carry Recipients in respect of such excess Carried Interest distributions (the "Tax Credit"), calculated using the highest marginal federal and applicable state income tax rates in effect in the applicable tax years in which such Carried Interest was distributed (the "Applicable Tax Rate"), multiplied by the amount of the Clawback Amount attributable to distributions made in such tax years, provided that (A) the Tax Credit shall be reduced to the extent that any Carry Recipient has received, or is entitled to receive, a tax refund or credit as a result of any loss or deduction arising from the repayment of any Clawback Amount pursuant to this Section 12.1, and (B) the Tax Credit shall in no event exceed the amount that would have been payable as income taxes on the Clawback Amount had such amount been distributed in the final year of the Fund; and (ii) any amounts held in the Escrow Account (as defined in Section 12.3) as of the date of determination, which amounts shall be first applied against the Clawback Amount (but without limiting the obligation of the Carry Recipients to fund any shortfall between the Clawback Amount, net of the Tax Credit, and the amounts held in the Escrow Account); provided further that the obligation to pay any Clawback Amount shall be subject to a statute of limitations of two (2) years following the later of (x) the date of the final liquidating distribution and (y) the date on which the Partnership's tax returns for the final fiscal year of the Partnership are filed (the "Clawback Limitation Date"), and any claim by the Partnership or any Limited Partner for payment of a Clawback Amount not made prior to the Clawback Limitation Date shall be forever barred; and provided further that no individual Carry Recipient shall be required to pay, in the aggregate, more than the net after-tax amount actually received by such Carry Recipient in respect of Carried Interest distributions.
